Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-18

Multireader is a document viewer. It is built so that your data stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Multireader does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data or document contents.

Internet access

On Android, Multireader does not request the internet permission, so it is technically unable to send data over the network. On iOS, the app makes no network requests.

Data on your device

To make reading convenient, Multireader keeps on your device only: the list of recently opened documents, your reading position in each of them, pinned documents and your theme preference, plus copies of documents opened from other apps (so they can be reopened from history). This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can remove any document from history at any time.

Third parties

Multireader contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. Crash reports, if any, are provided only by the operating system (Google Play Console / App Store Connect) under the settings you control on your device.

Children

Multireader does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
